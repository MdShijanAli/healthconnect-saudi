# MediConnect Pro

Create a modern, professional landing page for a Healthcare & Clinic Management SaaS Platform targeting clinics, doctors, and patients in Saudi Arabia (support for Arabic/English, RTL-ready layout).

Design style: Clean, trustworthy, medical-tech aesthetic. Use a blue and purple color palette (healthcare feel), soft shadows, rounded cards, plenty of white space. Modern sans-serif typography. Fully responsive.

Sections needed (in order):

1. Navbar - Logo, nav links (Features, Solutions, Pricing, About, Contact), language toggle (EN/AR), "Login" and "Get Started" buttons.

2. Hero Section - Bold headline about connecting clinics, doctors, and patients on one platform. Subtext explaining the value. Two CTA buttons: "Book a Demo" and "Get Started Free". Include a hero image placeholder showing a dashboard/app mockup.

3. Stats bar - 4 stat cards (e.g. "500+ Clinics", "50,000+ Patients", "99.9% Uptime", "24/7 Support").

4. Core Features grid - 8-9 feature cards with icons: Patient Management, Doctor Dashboard, Appointment Booking, EMR/Digital Records, E-Prescription, Lab & Pharmacy, Billing & Insurance, Telemedicine, Analytics.

5. Role-based tabs section - Three tabs: "For Clinics", "For Doctors", "For Patients" - each showing a short feature list and an image mockup.

6. Doctor Registration section - Explain onboarding process for doctors in 3 steps (Register → Verify → Start Consulting), with a "Join as a Provider" CTA button.

7. Patient Booking Flow section - 4-step visual flow (Register → Search Doctor → Book Appointment → Get Care), with "Book Appointment" CTA.

8. App Preview section - Mockup device frames showing mobile app and web dashboard screenshots.

9. Security & Compliance section - Highlight data encryption, role-based access control, audit logging, and compliance messaging.

10. Testimonials section - 3 testimonial cards with name, role, clinic, and quote (placeholder content).

11. Pricing section - 3 pricing tiers: Starter, Clinic, Enterprise, with feature checklists and "Choose Plan" buttons.

12. Integration partners logos section - Row of placeholder logos for insurance, payment, and lab system partners.

13. FAQ section - Accordion with 5-6 common questions about the platform.

14. Final CTA section - Full-width banner with headline and "Get Started" button.

15. Footer - Multi-column footer with company info, quick links, legal links, social icons, and newsletter signup.

Use consistent spacing, smooth hover animations on buttons and cards, and make the whole page feel premium and trustworthy — this is a healthcare product, so credibility and cleanliness matter more than flashy design.
